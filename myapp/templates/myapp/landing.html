End-To-End Architecture

How UCO-Connect Bridges the Last-Mile

A seamless, tamper-evident digital custody workflow from the kitchen fryer to the biofuel tank.

Stage 01

UCO Generator

Restaurant or vendor submits pickup request with estimated litres (2–3L), preferred time slot, and live GPS coordinates.

Stage 02

Micro-Clustering

Engine clusters 5–10 scattered nearby requests into an optimized TSP route (125–150L target) to cut fuel and travel time.

Stage 03

Verification Gate

Collector scans QR container, captures browser GPS, logs digital scale weight, uploads photo evidence, and triggers instant payout.

Stage 04

Depot Reconcile

Aggregation depot verifies collective weight, detects anomalies, applies digital security seal, and dispatches bulk batch.

Stage 05

Biodiesel Refinery

Authorized processor receives batch, verifies complete provenance chain, refines feedstock into clean B100 Biodiesel (~89% yield).

Solving the Grassroots Logistics Challenge

Why existing collection frameworks struggle with small FBOs and how UCO-Connect solves it.

Current Industry Bottleneck

Unviable economics & leakage risks

  • Tiny Volumes (2–3L/day): Individual pickups are economically unviable; route and fuel costs exceed oil value.
  • Manual Paper Records: No tamper-proof trail; quantity, location, and handover easily falsified.
  • Food Chain Leakage: High-TPC carcinogenic oil sold to informal street vendors or poured into municipal drains.
  • Fragmented Demand: Small food businesses have zero incentive or connection to authorized processors.

The UCO-Connect Solution

Hyperlocal clustering & verified digital custody

  • Micro-Collection Aggregation: Smart geo-clustering groups 5–10 tiny requests into one economical 125–150L run.
  • 5-Step Verification Gate: QR container scan + W3C GPS + scale weight + photo evidence creates immutable audit logs.
  • Instant UPI Payments: Guaranteed ₹50/L transparent payout settles instantly on verified collection.
  • RUCO Compliance Certificates: Automated digital disposal certificates prove diversion from the human food chain.
